# Transitioning from Shawn to Victoria
I want to preface this post with a brief overview of my life history in regards to my identity. I started my journey through this world with a birth certificate (though it was very delayed, maybe a sign) that listed me as Shawn Beauray Sagady with the gender marker *M* for male. This was in 1979 so there was little to zero acceptance of the idea of gender identity in western culture and medicine, dispite the fact that transgender people have existed long before western culture was ever a thing. One of the earliest known records of a transgender or third sex individual is found among the sexual depictions in Neolithic and Bronze Age drawings and figurines as early as 7,000 BCE[^Neolithihc]. The artwork depicts a human figure having female breasts and male genetals, or without distinguishing sex characteristics. 

Throughout history and around the world we find third sexy, two spirit or transgender individuals represented not only as regular members of society but often reverered or considered to be incredibly valuable to the societies social structure and balance. We are currently, in western civilizations, on the cusp of coming full circle to the acceptance and honoring of individuals that exist on the spectrum of gender and not just at the binary poles.  But do not confuse many cultures "acceptance" as being representative of equality or integration. The step of accepting trans people is the absolute bare minimum level of human decency and cannot be equated with a move away from discrimination, hate or resentment by society at large.

## Social Identity Stamps
So it was on some day in 1979 a member of the government in Santa Barbara, California stamped my birth certificate with an assignment of my identiy based on the assumptions of those who could not and would never know who I truly was, not for a very long time.
